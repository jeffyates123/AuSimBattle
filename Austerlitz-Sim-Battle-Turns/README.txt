AUSTERLITZ SIMULATED BATTLE COLLECTION
Collected 19 October 2026

56 original PDF/TXT files containing simulated battle setups or round-by-round results.
Two submitted battle order TXT files are in submitted-orders/.
Open index.html for battle numbers, content categories and links to the original Gmail messages.

PDF and TXT versions of the same turn are retained when available. Repeated email copies of the same named file appear once. Files are unmodified originals.
Battle numbers are local to each game. A resolved turn can also contain a later battle setup.
Tournament examples were recovered from Sim tournaments.zip and the nested Practice sim files.zip.

Searches covered all dates, Austerlitz PDF/TXT attachments, simulation terms, simulation filenames and mail from austerlitzpbem.com. Rulebooks, maps, strategy guides and turns without sim sections were excluded.
This is comprehensive for the searches performed; unusually named or unindexed messages may still be missed.
