Search and checks

Searched all dates for Austerlitz PDF/text attachments, simulated-battle terms, mail from austerlitzpbem.com, and simulation filenames. Checked the extracted contents of all retrieved PDF/text files. One scanned PDF was checked with OCR and contained a strategic map, not a simulated battle. Rulebooks, general strategy guidance, maps and ordinary turns without simulated-battle sections were excluded. Gmail search may miss unusually named files or unindexed material; this collection is comprehensive for the searches performed, not a guarantee that every possible email was found.
